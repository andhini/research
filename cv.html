About me

My research focuses on Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) and Fluid–Structure Interaction (FSI), high-fidelity simulations, and scalable numerical methods using immersed-boundary and Large-Eddy Simulation (LES). I am interested in techniques to model complex, unsteady flows with moving boundaries. By integrating novel methods to model complex flow into CFD, I aim to improve predictive accuracy and computational efficiency for applications in engineering and interdisciplinary research with life sciences. I like to do scuba diving, freediving and take underwater videography when I'm not at my desk.
