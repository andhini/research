About me

I investigate the physics of unsteady flows, transition to turbulence, and beyond, with particular interest in moving boundaries using high-fidelity simulation in immersed-boundary and Large-Eddy Simulation (LES). My work aims to improve the fidelity and computational efficiency, including scalable implementations for high-performance computing (HPC). By integrating novel computational methods, I aim to improve predictive accuracy and robustness for applications in engineering design, flow control, energy harvesting, and interdisciplinary research with life sciences. I am a certified Fellow of the Higher Education Academy (FHEA), a professional recognition for educators in UK higher education. I enjoy scuba diving, freediving, and underwater videography when I'm not at my desk.
