Profile

My research focuses on Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) and Fluid–Structure Interaction (FSI), high-fidelity simulation and scalable numerical methods on immersed-boundary and large-eddy simulation. I am interested in techniques to model complex, unsteady flows with moving boundaries. By integrating novel methods to model complex flow into CFD, I aim to improve predictive accuracy and computational efficiency for applications in engineering and interdisciplinary research with life sciences.
